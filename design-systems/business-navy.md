# Theme: Business Navy

## Direction

Professional B2B SaaS: reliable, structured, efficient, premium and
mature. Avoid playful, consumer-oriented, overly colorful or decorative
styling.

## Colors

Primary: #173B63 Primary Dark: #0F2742 Primary Light: #EAF1F8
Background: #F8FAFC Surface: #FFFFFF Surface Muted: #F1F5F9 Text:
#0F172A Text Secondary: #64748B Text Muted: #94A3B8 Border: #E2E8F0
Success: #16A34A Success Soft: #DCFCE7 Warning: #D97706 Warning Soft:
#FEF3C7 Error: #DC2626 Error Soft: #FEE2E2 Info: #2563EB Info Soft:
#DBEAFE

## Typography

Font: Inter. Weights 400, 500, 600, 700.

H1 28/36, H2 22/30, H3 18/26, Body 14/20, Body Large 16/24, Small 12/16.

## Spacing

4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48px.

## Radius

6, 8, 10, 12px. Default 8px. Cards 10px. Inputs/buttons 8px. Avoid 20px+
except pills/avatars.

## Borders and shadows

Default border: 1px solid #E2E8F0.

Default cards have no shadow. Use subtle shadows only for dropdowns,
popovers, modals and floating elements.

## Components

Cards are white, bordered and compact. Tables are first-class: clear
headers, compact rows, subtle separators, right-aligned numbers and
restrained hover states.

Forms should feel precise. Inputs are around 40px high.

Buttons: primary navy/white, secondary white/navy/border, ghost navy,
destructive red.

## Navigation

Prefer sidebar or top navigation. White/slightly tinted surface, subtle
border, light navy active state.

## Nuxt UI mapping

primary → #173B63 neutral → slate success → green warning → amber error
→ red info → blue

## Visual goal

Premium B2B SaaS dashboard. Communicate trust, stability, precision and
competence.
