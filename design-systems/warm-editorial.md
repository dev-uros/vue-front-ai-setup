# Theme: Warm Editorial

## Direction

Warm, human and content-focused. Suitable for content platforms,
education, publishing, communities, lifestyle and knowledge products.

Feel human, warm, thoughtful, calm and crafted. Avoid childish, neon,
cold or aggressively gamified styling.

## Colors

Primary: #B4532A Primary Dark: #7C321F Primary Light: #FCEDE6
Background: #FAF8F5 Surface: #FFFFFF Surface Warm: #F5F0E8 Text: #292524
Text Secondary: #78716C Text Muted: #A8A29E Border: #E7E0D8 Success:
#3F8F5A Warning: #C47A22 Error: #C94A4A Info: #5277A8

## Typography
Display font: Fraunces (H1, H2, editorial headings) — variable, weight 500-600,
optical size on. This is a required dependency, not decoration.
Body font: Source Sans 3.
H1 34/42 Fraunces, H2 26/34 Fraunces, H3 20/28 Source Sans 600,
Body 17/27, Secondary 15/23.

## Spacing

4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px.

Editorial sections benefit from generous vertical spacing.

## Radius
Inputs/buttons 8px. Cards 12px. Featured 16px. Pills 9999px.

## Borders and shadows

Default border: 1px solid #E7E0D8.

Default shadow: none.

Featured/floating surfaces may use: 0 6px 24px rgba(41,37,36,0.08)

## Components

Cards prioritize content: strong title, metadata, readable excerpt,
optional image, subtle border and restrained radius.

Primary CTA: warm terracotta. Secondary: warm neutral surface + dark
text.

Tags use warm neutral backgrounds by default. Semantic colors are
reserved for actual states.

Images should carry visual personality. Prefer large editorial imagery,
generous cropping and consistent aspect ratios.

## Nuxt UI mapping

primary → #B4532A neutral → stone success → green warning → amber error
→ red info → blue

Prefer UCard, UButton, UBadge, UInput, UTabs, UAvatar, UModal,
UDropdownMenu and UAlert.

## Visual goal

A thoughtful premium content product where typography, spacing and
imagery do most of the visual work.
